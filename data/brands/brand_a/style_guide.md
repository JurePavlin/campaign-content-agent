# Moien Vëlo: style guide

> **Synthetic.** Moien Vëlo is a fictional company created for this portfolio project. Its products, people, customers, claims and evidence are invented.

## Who we are

Moien Vëlo is a small Luxembourg company, based in Bertrange, that makes electric bikes which are **slow on purpose**. Every bike's motor stops helping at 20 km/h or less, well below the EU limit of 25 km/h, because a slower bike needs less distance to stop and gives the rider more time to see, think and react.

**The name:** *Moien* is the everyday Luxembourgish greeting ("hello") and *Vëlo* means bicycle, so the name is a friendly "hello, bike". Always write it with the ë. In running text, "a Moien Vëlo bike" is fine, even though the name already contains "bike".

**Tagline:** *We don't want to get you there fast. We want to get you there alive.*

**What makes us different:** other e-bike brands compete on speed and range. We compete on confidence. Our bikes are built to be slow because it makes them safer, and we say so plainly.

## Who we write for

- People in later life, mostly 60 and over, who are comfortable financially. Many have retired from professional careers in finance, the civil service or the EU institutions, and many live across the border in France, Belgium or Germany.
- They value reliability, good service and being treated as intelligent adults. They read carefully and distrust hype.
- They want to stay active and independent. Quite a few of them also enjoy, quietly, that the motor does most of the work. We share the joke with them; we never make them the joke.

**The golden rule: we joke about effort, never about age.** No jokes about frailty, memory, hearing or "senior moments". We don't label readers by age at all: we write to riders, customers and people, not to "the elderly".

## Voice

We are four things, and we are careful not to tip into their opposites.

| We are | We are not | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Reassuring** | Patronising | Explain once, clearly. Never "don't worry, it's easy, even for you". |
| **Dry-witted** | Jokey | At most one understated joke per piece, never at the reader's expense. No puns stacked on puns. |
| **Courteous** | Stuffy | Polite, complete sentences with "we" and "you". No legal language, no "hereby". |
| **Honest** | Salesy | Say what the bike doesn't do. Never pressure people to buy. |

**Values:** safety over speed · plain language · honest about trade-offs · respect for the reader's experience.

## Tone by channel

Each score runs from 0 to 1 on the four Nielsen Norman Group tone dimensions: 0 is the first word, 1 the second.

| Dimension | Social (LinkedIn) | Email (newsletter) | Web (product pages) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Funny ↔ serious | 0.4 | 0.5 | 0.7 |
| Formal ↔ casual | 0.5 | 0.4 | 0.3 |
| Respectful ↔ irreverent | 0.2 | 0.1 | 0.1 |
| Enthusiastic ↔ matter-of-fact | 0.6 | 0.6 | 0.8 |

Social can smile; the website is calm and factual; email sits in between.

## Style rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Spelling | British English (en-GB): colour, tyre, centre, organise, pedalled |
| Reading level | Flesch-Kincaid grade 8 or lower |
| Sentences | Short. Aim for an average under 20 words |
| Contractions | Allowed (we're, you'll, don't). No slang contractions such as "gonna" |
| Apostrophes | Straight (`'`), not curly (`’`), so text matches the glossary exactly |
| Emoji | Social: at most 1. Email and web: none |
| Exclamation marks | Social and email: at most 1. Web: none. Most pieces need none |
| Hashtags | Social only, at most 3, at the end |
| Length | Social 40–150 words · Email 60–200 words · Web 30–120 words |
| Numbers | Digits with a space before the unit: 15 km/h, 24 kg, 60 km, 400 Wh |
| Prices | Euro sign first, comma for thousands: €2,890 |
| Dates | 2 November 2026 |

## Words

### Banned

| Group | Words | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Internet slang | skibidi, low key, lowkey, no cap, rizz, slay, bussin, it's giving, vibe check, vibes, sus, fr fr, bestie, periodt, yeet, sheesh, delulu, main character energy, on fleek | Wrong voice for our readers, and dates quickly |
| Hype and speed boasts | revolutionary, world-class, game-changer, cutting-edge, next-level, unleash, turbo, blazing, lightning-fast, adrenaline, need for speed | Contradicts who we are. We are not fast and we don't shout |
| Generic environmental claims | eco-friendly, environmentally friendly, climate neutral, carbon neutral, climate positive, planet-friendly, green credentials | Banned in EU advertising since 27 September 2026 (Directive (EU) 2024/825) unless proven to a high standard. We give specific facts instead |
| Labels that patronise | elderly, OAP, pensioner, pensioners, old folks, geriatric, senior moment, over the hill, young at heart, silver surfer | We never label or talk down to our readers |

## Claims

**If it isn't in the claims register (`claims.yaml`) or the product catalogue (`catalogue.yaml`), we don't say it.** That covers every fact about speed, range, weight, price, safety, health, the environment and our services.

- **Safety:** we may say our bikes stop helping at lower speeds and that a slower bike needs less distance to stop. We may not say we're "the safest", or put a number on accident risk.
- **Health:** we may say you always pedal and the motor adds to your effort. We may not promise health outcomes (blood pressure, weight loss, calories).
- **Environment:** only specific, evidenced facts (recycled aluminium content, battery take-back). No generic claims and nothing based on carbon offsetting.
- **Offers and prices:** check the dates. An offer that has expired is not a claim we can make.
